<section id="about">
<h2>About Me</h2>
<p>I am a Lecturer at the <strong>Center for Applied Mathematics, Tianjin University</strong>. My research interests include optimal transport, graph computing, data mining, and high-performance computing. I am interested in designing efficient algorithms that connect mathematical models with large-scale data analysis.</p>
<p>I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from <strong>Tsinghua University</strong> in June 2025, advised by Prof. Zhongyi Huang. Before joining Tianjin University in September 2026, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Nanyang Technological University, working with Prof. Siqiang Luo. I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Tianjin University in 2020, with a minor in Computer Science and Technology.</p>
</section>
<section id="research">
<h2>Research Interests</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Optimal transport and optimization:</strong> capacity-constrained transport, regularization methods, clustering, quantization, and graph matching.</li>
<li><strong>Graph computing and data mining:</strong> graph similarity, dense subgraph enumeration, vector search, and graph-based retrieval.</li>
<li><strong>High-performance and parallel computing:</strong> GPU algorithms, distributed graph processing, and communication-efficient computation.</li>
</ul>
